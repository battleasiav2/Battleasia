TOURNAMENT RULES

Read once, play forever. Every lobby runs on the same fair-play contract.

Fair play & anti-cheat
Any aim assist, emulator on mobile-only lobbies, or third-party software results in an instant ban and forfeited entry. Matches are recorded and reviewed on flag.
Join time & lobby lock
Lobbies lock 5 minutes before start. Room ID and password are pushed to your dashboard and Discord DM. Late joins forfeit entry with no refund.
In-game name must match
Your in-game name must exactly match your BattleAsia profile. Mismatched names are removed from the lobby before payouts are calculated.
Prizes & distribution
Prize pools are split by placement and kills as shown on each card. BAC is credited within 10 minutes of result verification.
Teaming & collusion
Solo and Duo teaming with other squads is prohibited. Confirmed collusion voids winnings for all involved accounts.
Payments & entry fees
Entry is paid in BAC, topped up via bKash, Nagad or crypto. Fees are shown before you confirm — no hidden charges.
Disconnects & reconnects
You may reconnect within the match timer. BattleAsia is not responsible for personal network loss; server-side outages are refunded in full.
Abuse & toxicity
Harassment, slurs or threats in chat or voice lead to escalating suspensions. We keep the arena competitive, not hostile.
Payouts & withdrawals
Withdraw any time above the 100 BAC minimum. First withdrawal requires one-time KYC; after that, cash-outs settle in under an hour.
Final decision authority
In disputes, the BattleAsia officials' ruling is final. Evidence submissions close 30 minutes after match end.

Questions?

Our support relay is live around the clock across every region.
